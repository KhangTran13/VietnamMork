Who We Want

We want a proven owner-type ecommerce operator, not a corporate administrator. The ideal candidate has already run, built or materially scaled an ecommerce, DTC or direct-response consumer-products business.

Strong candidates will have:

  • Senior leadership experience
  • Direct P&L responsibility
  • Proven ecommerce/DTC operating experience
  • Strong online performance-marketing knowledge
  • Experience launching and scaling products
